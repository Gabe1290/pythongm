Phase 3: The Status Bar

Corner text is fine, but classic shooters keep a solid bar along the bottom. There are two steps: make room, then draw the bar.

Step 1: Make Room

Our room is 320 pixels tall and we want a 64-pixel bar. So the 3D view gets 320 − 64 = 256 pixels. In obj_player's When created event, set Viewport Height on Enable Raycast View:

When created
Enable Raycast View — … Viewport Height 256

Left at 0, the 3D view fills the whole window and the bar would cover part of it.

Step 2: Give the Player Health

In Game Start, add Set health = 100 after the score and lives. The bar shows this number.

Step 3: Draw the Bar

In the Draw event, replace the score and lives with one action:

Draw
Draw DOOM HUD — X 0 Y -1 Width 0 Height 64 Health Label HEALTH Score Label SCORE
Draw Minimap — X 230 Y 10 Size 80
  • Y = -1 means "put the bar at the bottom of the window"
  • Width = 0 means "full width"
  • Height must match the band you reserved (64)

Test It!

Press F5!

  • The 3D view now sits above a dark bar showing a green health bar with its number, your score and your lives
  • The minimap is still in the corner of the 3D view

Challenges

  • Make the monster take 25 health (Set health −25 relative) instead of a life, and refill health when a life is lost — like raycast_3
  • Draw a face in the bar: a sprite strip of four faces, healthiest first, set as Face Sprite with Face Frames = 4. The face changes as health drops
  • The bar has a counter labelled Objective Label ("Keys: "): count your gems in an instance variable and give its name to Objective Value

You Finished the Series!

  • Draw events put the HUD over the 3D view
  • Draw Minimap and Draw DOOM HUD do the heavy lifting
  • Viewport Height reserves room for a bar
