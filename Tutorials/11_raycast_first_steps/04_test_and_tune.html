Phase 3: Test and Tune

Test Your Game!

Press F5 to play!

  • The room appears in first person — grey walls, a sky-blue ceiling and a dark floor
  • Left and Right arrows turn you; Up and Down walk
  • You bump into the border walls and the three blocks in the middle instead of walking through them

If Something Looks Wrong

What you seeLikely cause
The normal top-down roomThe Create event with Enable Raycast View is missing, or is on an object that isn't in the room
You walk through wallsThe Collision with obj_wall event is missing, or obj_wall is not Solid
Walls look wrong or gaps appearA wall block is off the 32-pixel grid, or Cell Size is not 32
You turn the wrong waySwap the 3 and -3 on the two turning events

Experiment

Change one thing at a time on Enable Raycast View and press F5 again:

  • Field of View — try 40 (like a telescope) and 100 (very wide, a little fish-eyed)
  • Render Distance — try 3: far walls simply vanish into the background
  • Wall Color, Floor Color, Ceiling Color — make a dungeon or a Mars base
  • Columns — fewer columns draw faster but look chunkier

You Built Your First 2.5D World!

  • Solid blocks on a 32-pixel grid become walls
  • The player object is the camera
  • One facing angle drives both what you see and where you walk
