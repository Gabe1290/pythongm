2.5D: Textures, Sky and Floor

🧱

From flat grey boxes to a real brick dungeon!

Paint the walls, add a sky that turns as you turn, and lay a tiled stone floor.

What We'll Build

We take the room from 2.5D: First Steps and dress it up. Nothing about how the game plays changes — only what it looks like. If you no longer have that project, finish Lesson 11 first (about 20 minutes).

How This Tutorial Works

Phase 1 — Textured Walls
Draw a brick picture and wrap every wall in it.
Phase 2 — Sky and Floor
Add a panoramic sky and a tiled floor.
Phase 3 — Tune the Look
Switch textures on and off, and trade detail for speed.

What You'll Learn

  • How a texture is just a sprite chosen in Enable Raycast View
  • What the sky does when you turn (and why it never gets closer)
  • How the floor is drawn, and what Floor Detail trades away
  • How to fall back to plain colours
Time Required: About 20–25 minutes.
