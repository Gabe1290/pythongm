Phase 3: Tune the Look

Everything Has a Plain-Colour Fallback

TextureColour used when it is empty
Wall TextureWall Color (default dark red)
Floor TextureFloor Color (default dark olive)
Sky Texture / Ceiling TextureCeiling Color (default sky blue)

Try it: clear Sky Texture and press F5 — you get the flat blue ceiling back, and nothing else changes. Set Textured Walls to off to see flat wall colours even though a Wall Texture is still set — handy for comparing.

Detail Versus Speed

A 2.5D view is drawn column by column, and the floor is the most expensive part. Two settings control the trade:

SettingLower meansHigher means
Columns (default 320)Chunkier walls, fasterSmoother walls, slower
Floor Detail (default 4)Sharper floor, slowerBlockier floor, faster
Rule of thumb: if your game feels slow, raise Floor Detail to 6 or 8 first — you will barely notice the difference — then lower Columns. If it looks too blocky, go the other way.
