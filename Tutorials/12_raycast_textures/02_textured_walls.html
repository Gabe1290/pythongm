Phase 1: Textured Walls

A texture is nothing special: it is an ordinary sprite. The 2.5D view cuts it into thin vertical strips and stretches each strip onto the wall in front of it — taller when the wall is close, shorter when it is far away.

Draw the Brick Sprite

  1. Create a sprite named spr_wall_texture
  2. In the Sprite Editor, make it 64×64 pixels. Fill it with a brick red, then draw light mortar lines: a horizontal line every 16 pixels, and short vertical lines between the bricks, shifted by half a brick on every other row.
  3. Save with Ctrl+S

Prefer a photo or a picture from the internet? Use Import Image... instead. Any picture works — square ones (like 64×64) look best because the texture is repeated the same way on every wall.

Give It to the Walls

  1. Open obj_player → Blockly tab → its When created event
  2. On Enable Raycast View, set Wall Texture to spr_wall_texture
When created
Enable Raycast View — Field of View 66 Cell Size 32 Render Distance 20 Wall Texture spr_wall_texture

Test It!

Press F5!

  • Every wall is now covered in bricks
  • Walls facing away from the light look darker — the game shades the two directions differently, a cheap trick that makes corners look solid
  • Walk up close: the bricks grow; step back: they shrink
