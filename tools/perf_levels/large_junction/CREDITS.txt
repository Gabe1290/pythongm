sprites/spr_wall.png, spr_low.png, spr_junction.png, spr_gate.png and
spr_step_*.png are trivial generated placeholders (a flat coloured square with a
darker border, written by tools/gen_raycast_6_junction.py) -- not derived from
any licensed artwork, no license notice needed. They are never drawn in the
first-person view; they only show where walls, steps, ceilings, the marker and
the gates are in the room editor.
