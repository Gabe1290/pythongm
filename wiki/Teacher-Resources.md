# Teacher Resources for the PyGameMaker Tutorials

*[Home](Home)*

---

Everything a teacher needs to run the in-app tutorials (**Help > Tutorials**) with a class: a printable **student handout**, a **worksheet** with self-check questions, and a **teacher guide** (timing, common mistakes, answer key, rubric, extension ideas) for each tutorial. English and French.

## How to use this material

1. Read the teacher guide of the lesson before class; do the build-along yourself once.
2. Give students the handout (print the PDF, or let them open the wiki page next to the tutorial).
3. Use the worksheet as an exit ticket or homework; the answers are in the teacher guide.
4. Grade or discuss the finished project with the rubric at the end of the teacher guide.

> INFO: The **beginner edition** of PyGameMaker only shows tutorials 1-4. To teach later lessons, switch the edition in the preferences before class.

## Suggested sequence

| Step | Tutorial | New ideas | Needs first | Time |
| 1 | Getting Started | interface, sprite, object, room, F5 | nothing | 45 min |
| 2 | First Game: Catch the Star | keyboard event, movement, collisions, score | 1 | 45-60 min |
| 3 | Catch the Coins: Win and Lose | win and lose conditions, game over | 2 | 45 min |
| 4 | Classic Pong | two players, bouncing, scoring | 2 | 60 min |
| 5 | Breakout | many objects, bouncing off bricks | 4 | 60 min |
| 6 | Maze | walls, coins, exit | 2 | 45-60 min |
| 7 | Sokoban | pushing, grid logic | 6 | 45-60 min |
| 8 | Platformer | gravity, jumping | 6 | 60 min |
| 9 | Lunar Lander | thrust, fuel | 8 | 45-60 min |
| 10 | Turn-Based Multiplayer (file exchange) | two computers, turns | 4 | 60 min + lab setup |
| 11-14 | 2.5D series: First Steps, Textures, Goals and Monsters, HUD and Minimap | first-person view, textures, billboards, HUD | 6 | 45 min each |

> TIP: Times are suggestions for a class doing the whole lesson; each teacher guide has a shortened variant for a 45-minute slot. Fast students can go on with the challenges printed on the handout.

## Assessment

Each teacher guide ends with a four-level rubric for the finished project and an answer key for the worksheet. A simple course-long approach: keep every student's project from lessons 1, 2, 4 and 6 in a folder and compare them at the end of term.
